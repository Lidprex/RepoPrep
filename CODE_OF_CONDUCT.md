# Code of Conduct

## Our community standard

The RepoPrep project — its issue tracker, pull requests, and any discussions
around it — is open to anyone who gives it a chance, regardless of background,
experience level, language, or opinion about the best way to rename a lock
file. Everyone should be able to participate without being attacked for their
views or their knowledge.

## What's fine

- Helpful, respectful feedback — on code, translations, and documentation
- Constructive criticism that names the problem, not the person
- Patience with newcomers; RepoPrep has contributors at very different levels
- Focusing on the project's goals: a better tool for preparing codebases

## What isn't

- Insults, trolling, or personal attacks
- Posting someone else's private information without permission
- Harassment of any form, sexualized or otherwise

## Enforcement

Maintainers will step in when this standard is crossed, and will deal with
issues proportionately — a warning where it fits, removal or a ban where it
doesn't. Reports are handled confidentially.

If you run into behavior that shouldn't be happening, tell us:

- support.lidprex@gmail.com

We take these reports seriously and will review them promptly.

## Scope

This applies everywhere the project operates publicly — mainly the GitHub
repository and the Lidprex support channels.
